🎮 What Is Rig Picker?

Rig Picker is a Blender add-on that lets you **select character rig controls through a visual picker** instead of searching through bones in a cluttered 3D viewport.

**Clean viewport. Clear character views. Organized controls.**

👥 Who Is Rig Picker For?

Rig Picker is for character animators, rigging artists, and anyone working with character rigs in Blender who wants a cleaner viewport and an easier way to select controls.

✨ Why Use Rig Picker?
- 🎛️ Dedicated Picker Interface — Access rig controls through a custom UI.
- 🎬 Animation-Focused Workflow — Reduce friction when working with character rigs.
- ⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts — Use supported hotkeys for quicker access to common actions.
- 🧹 Less Viewport Clutter — Keep your Blender viewport cleaner by accessing rig controls through a dedicated picker interface.
- 🧩 Blender-Native Experience — Looks and feels almost like Blender’s native interface.


📸 **Effortless Image Capture** — Capture images for your visual picker with ease.
🎯 **Precision Control Placement** — Position controls exactly where you want them on your rig images.
🔥 **Dynamic Panel Management** — Organize each rig into multiple panels for easy access.
↔️ **Exceptional Symmetry Support** — Mirror controls across both sides of your rig with ease.
⌨️ **Supercharged Hotkeys** — Speed up your workflow with convenient keyboard shortcuts.
⚙️ **Seamless Custom Actions** — Create custom buttons and toggles to access rig operations directly from the visual picker.


✨ Key Features at a Glance

🎯 **Easy Control Selection** — Keep your viewport clutter-free by selecting character rig controls directly through the visual picker.

🎭 **Multiple Rig Support** — Manage controls for different character rigs within the visual picker.

📂 **Organized Panels for Your Rigs** — Group controls into panels such as Body, Face, and Fingers for easy access.

↔️ **Symmetry Support** — Add controls on both sides of your rig faster with symmetry.

⌨️ **Powerful Hotkeys** — Speed up your workflow with convenient keyboard shortcuts.

⚡ **Custom Buttons & Toggles** — Create custom buttons and toggles to execute Python commands for rig operations, such as IK-FK switching, snapping, and more.
